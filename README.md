Build polished CVs faster with a modern, smart, and beautifully structured experience bec some kid dev decdied to do a site for u for that :)

## what is it?
CraftCV is a small and light CV builder designed to help job seekers or uni students or anyone who wanna try and look how his info looks on a real cv create cool or professional whatever u name it resumes quickly and easliy bec It solves the common problem of making and presenting career information clearly just by turning a simple form into a pretty polished, live-updating CV preview and plus its all in the browser plus its with zero setup and no backend required (for now)

##  How to Use?
pretty easy its as  follows: 

after opening this -https://assembakr00.github.io/CraftCV/
first after u open it u can Open Settings from the menu to choose a language (English or Arabic), a font, the CV section order, or add your API key for AI Enhance then Fill in your personal details, experience, education, projects, and skills.
and ofc u can Watch the live preview update on the right as you type.
then Pick a template above the preview, then export, share, or copy your CV whenever you are ready.

Use Restart Demo in Settings to clear all saved browser data, including your API key.
