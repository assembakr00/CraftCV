Build polished CVs faster with a modern, smart, and beautifully structured experience bec some kid dev decdied to do a site for u for that :)

## what is it?
CraftCV is a smal designed to help job seekers or student or ppl who jsut wanna see their cvs without much work create prof. resumes quickly and confidently It solves the common problem of formatting and presenting career information clearly by turning a simple form into a cool and polished live-updating CV preview and all in the browser plus its with zero setup and no backend required (for now).

##  How to Use?
1. Open the app in your browser ofc
Open Settings from the menu to choose a language (English or Arabic), a font, the CV section order, or add your API key for AI Enhance pls check it ou i worked a lot on it and then ofc 
Fill in your personal details, experience, education, projects, and skills and u can
Use AI Enhance on any section to polish your text with your LLM (if u added an api) and
Watch the live preview update on the right as you type
Pick a template above the preview, then export, share, or copy your CV whenever you are ready
also the check and preview btn just check if smth is missing

Use **Restart Demo** in Settings to clear all saved browser data, including your API key.
