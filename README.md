## what is it?
CraftCV is a lightweight CV builder designed to help job seekers create professional resumes quickly and confidently. It solves the common problem of formatting and presenting career information clearly by turning a simple form into a polished, live-updating CV preview — all in the browser plus its with zero setup and no backend required (for now).

##  How to Use?
1. Open the app in your browser.
2. (Optional) Add your API key in the settings bar at the top to enable AI Enhance.
3. Fill in your personal details, experience, education, projects, and skills.
4. Use AI Enhance on any section to polish your text with your LLM if u added an api
5. Watch the live preview update on the right as you type.
6. Export, share, or copy your CV whenever you are ready.
